Each composition is one allowed vehicle selection. XTM copies the group's ordered composition list to every member line, so multiple compositions let the game choose among those entries.

Most passenger and cargo modes use one primary vehicle. Cargo Train, Tram, and Subway groups can pair a primary engine with an optional secondary carriage. Passenger rail models are treated as self-contained vehicles.

Cards show the localized model name, thumbnail, capacity and, where available, length. Cargo rail cards can also show unit count and calculated combined length.

Select an already selected card to clear that part of a composition, or remove the whole composition with its trash button.
