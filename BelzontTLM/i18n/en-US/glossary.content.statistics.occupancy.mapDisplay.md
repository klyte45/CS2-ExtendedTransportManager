The XTM linear-map toolbar has separate controls for **Platform crowding** and **Segment occupancy**.

Open the Segment occupancy menu to choose **None**, **Current hour**, **Daily average**, or one of six four-hour periods. Segment percentages appear between the departure stop and the next stop. Higher values use stronger backgrounds and values of 75% or more pulse.

In symmetric half-trip mode, down and up arrows distinguish the two directions. Select a percentage to open that directed segment's full history.

Platform crowding changes stop markers using live waiting load. It can be displayed together with historical segment occupancy. Both overlays start enabled, with segment occupancy set to Current hour.

Stale or unavailable periods appear as 0% on the map. Open the segment chart to distinguish complete history from gaps.
