Every vehicle model group belongs to one transport type and to either passenger lines or cargo routes. Choose this scope before creating the group. The menu only lists combinations currently represented by lines in the city.

The scope cannot change after creation. Create another group when passenger and cargo fleets, or two transport modes, need different selections.

Scope controls both the available vehicle models and lines shown in the editor. A line's Selected Info Panel offers only groups matching its exact transport type and passenger/cargo category.
