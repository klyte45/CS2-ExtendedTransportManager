Each step contains one or more **keyframes**. Dynamic blinds cycle them for their configured simulation-frame durations. Static blinds use only the selected static keyframe.

**Route name** shows the route's display name. **Destination stop** shows the step boundary, or the first stop for end-of-line. **Route number** uses the effective display identifier or internal number. **Static text** shows fixed text. **Next stop** follows the vehicle. **Destination stop or district or Road** falls back through the stop's attached district, road, or aggregate and is the default.

Choose **Keyframe to use as static blind** for non-animated layouts. A step with one keyframe naturally stays on that value in dynamic layouts.
