Open a line's vehicle-selection section and use its gear menu. An unmanaged line can join any group with the same transport type and passenger/cargo category, or open **Manage vehicle model groups**.

A managed line shows its group name and affected-line count. Its menu can edit the group, remove this line, or move it to another compatible group.

In the group editor, add or remove individual lines, remove all members, or add every currently filtered available line. Activity filters include disabled, day-and-night, day-only, and night-only lines; inactive lines remain assignable.

Lines assigned elsewhere are visible by default with a warning. Adding one moves it from its former group. Hide assigned lines before bulk adding when those memberships should remain unchanged.

A group can retain its compositions without any linked lines.
