Select a transport route to open its **Selected Info Panel**. You can also press **Details** on an XTM listing card or select a route shield in an occupancy report.

## Selected route

XTM replaces the route's Color section with controls for a palette-generated or fixed color. The Ticket Price and vehicle-selection sections gain group controls: assign the route, move it to another group, remove it from a group, edit its current group, or open the corresponding management screen in Transportation Overview.

The **Line Data** section edits the internal route number and optional display identifier. The display identifier overrides the number on generated names and shields. Expand **Advanced data** for waiting and loaded totals, estimated lap time, maintenance information, average occupancy, and historical occupancy.

When Write Everywhere is installed and available, its button in Line Data opens destination-blind editing.

## Selected stop

Select a stop in the XTM linear map to see Stop Data. From there you can focus its parent building, switch to the opposite platform when one exists, make the stop the first stop of the route, and inspect the approaching vehicle.

## Selected vehicle

Enable vehicles on the XTM linear map and select a vehicle to see its odometer and maintenance interval.

## Selection limits

Color, fare-group, vehicle-group, identity, advanced-data, and destination-blind controls belong to the selected route. Stop and vehicle selections receive their own smaller Line Data sections instead.
