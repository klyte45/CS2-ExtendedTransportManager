XTM destination blinds require **Write Everywhere** (`BelzontWE`). When that mod is present and XTM can attach its integration hooks, blinds are available for every transport line.

Select a route and open **Line Data**. The Write Everywhere button opens the draggable **Write Everywhere - Custom blinds** window. The button is not shown for stop or vehicle selections.

Without Write Everywhere, the button and destination-blind editing are unavailable. This whole **Write Everywhere** Encyclopedia category is also omitted.
