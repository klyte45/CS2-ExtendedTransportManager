Open the game's **Transportation Overview**, then use the XTM button in the panel header to switch between the original overview and XTM's management interface.

## XTM screens

Press **Change mode** to choose **Line listing**, **Fare Groups**, **Vehicle Model Groups**, **Available palettes**, **Occupancy Report: Passengers**, or **Occupancy Report: Cargo**. The chosen XTM screen, listing filters, and sorting are remembered during the current frontend session. Changing to another XTM screen clears the listing filters.

## Line listing

Use the transport-type and activity buttons to filter the list. **Show all**, **Hide all**, **Passenger lines**, and **Cargo routes** provide quick filter presets. The sort menu orders routes by number, acronym, length, usage, monthly passenger or cargo volume, or schedule.

Each card shows its route identity, name, type, length, monthly volume, vehicle count, occupancy range, and schedule. Select its name, identifier, shield, color, or schedule controls to edit those values directly. Press **Details** to select the route and open its Selected Info Panel.

## Reports and management screens

Passenger and cargo occupancy reports rank lines and individual segments overall and in six four-hour periods. Use a column heading for a full ranking, reverse the order with the sort control, or request a new snapshot with **Refresh data**.

Fare Groups, Vehicle Model Groups, and Available palettes contain their shared-management tools. Group controls in a route's Selected Info Panel can also open Transportation Overview directly on the matching group screen.

## Defaults and limits

**Use XTM line listing by default** is enabled by default in the mod options. The header button can still return to the original overview for the current panel session.

If the city has no transport lines, only Line listing and Available palettes are selectable through **Change mode**. Occupancy reports require recorded occupancy data.
