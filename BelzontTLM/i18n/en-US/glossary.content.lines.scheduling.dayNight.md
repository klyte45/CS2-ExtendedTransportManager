## Continuous service

**Day & night** keeps the route enabled during both scheduling periods. It is the default classification when an active route has neither the Day nor Night option.

Select the first button in a card's schedule strip to enable the route and apply Day & night service. Selecting the button again while it is already current makes no change.

## How it appears

Day-and-night cards use the normal light card text and a green selected schedule button. Their route shields do not receive a service-state badge.

![Cards in all four service states, with the day-and-night button highlighted on the first](coui://xtm.k45/UI/images/xtm-line-card-anatomy.jpg)

Use the **Day & night** toolbar button to include or exclude these routes from the listing. Service-state filters combine with transport-type filters, so a card must pass both.

![Scheduling filters: disabled, day-and-night, day only and night only](coui://xtm.k45/UI/images/xtm-listing-service-states.jpg)
