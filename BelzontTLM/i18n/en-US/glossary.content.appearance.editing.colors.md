Select a city palette to edit its sequence. **Add color** appends white. Select a numbered swatch to open the color wheel and RGB controls, or its X to remove it.

Drag swatches to reorder them, or choose **Shuffle colors**. Order matters because automatic coloring selects by position.

**Append palette** adds colors from a disk file or bundled preset. A palette holds at most 500 colors; excess additions are omitted.

Color edits, removals, reordering, shuffling, and appending remain temporary until **Save changes**. **Reset changes** restores saved colors. Renaming saves separately; deletion cannot be undone.
