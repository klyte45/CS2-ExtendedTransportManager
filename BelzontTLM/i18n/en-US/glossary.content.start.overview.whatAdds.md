Extended Transport Manager expands the game's existing transport screens rather than adding a separate management window. It provides a detailed listing for every passenger line and cargo route, with transport-type and service filters, sorting, inline identity and color controls, and direct access to route details.

XTM also records historical occupancy. The passenger and cargo reports rank lines and route segments, while the enhanced linear route map can display waiting load, vehicle positions, interchanges, districts, distances, and historical segment occupancy.

**Fare Groups** let passenger lines share a default fare and optional hourly exceptions. **Vehicle Model Groups** let compatible passenger lines or cargo routes share an allowed fleet. **Palettes** assign colors separately for each passenger or cargo transport type, while an individual route can keep a fixed-color override.

Selecting a route, stop, or vehicle reveals additional information in the Selected Info Panel. XTM adds route identifiers and generated shields, group assignment controls, trip and maintenance data, and optional Write Everywhere destination-blind tools.
