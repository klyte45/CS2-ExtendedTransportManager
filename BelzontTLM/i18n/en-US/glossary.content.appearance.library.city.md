City palettes belong to the current city or editor world and are stored in its save. Only city palettes can be assigned to transport types for automatic coloring.

The list is sorted by name. Select a palette to inspect or edit it. A new palette starts with one white color. Imported palettes receive their own identity, so duplicate names are allowed.

Deleting a city palette cannot be undone. If it was assigned to a transport type, choose another palette or explicitly disable automatic coloring for that type.
