Open **Vehicle Model Groups**, select a transport type, and choose **Add vehicle model group**. A new group opens with the localized unnamed label.

Add at least one valid composition before other changes can save. Valid changes then save automatically. Names accept up to 64 characters; a blank name becomes the unnamed label. A valid group may have no linked lines.

Use the group card's trash button and confirm deletion. Member lines return to ordinary vehicle-selection controls, but their last applied model list remains until changed normally or by another group.
