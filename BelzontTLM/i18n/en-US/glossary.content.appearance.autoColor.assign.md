The lower part of **Available palettes** assigns one city palette to each supported transport type. Passenger and cargo assignments are independent.

Passenger assignments support bus, tram, subway, train, ship, airplane, and ferry. Cargo assignments support train, ship, and airplane.

Select a transport type and choose a city palette, or choose **Auto color disabled**. Disabling does not select a replacement color, so routes may retain their current colors.

An individual route with **Use fixed color** enabled takes priority over its transport-type assignment.
