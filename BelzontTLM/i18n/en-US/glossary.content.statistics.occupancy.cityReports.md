Open XTM's Transportation Overview and choose **Occupancy Report: Passengers** or **Occupancy Report: Cargo** through **Change mode**. The report is a snapshot; use **Refresh data** to generate another. Inactive routes are omitted.

Both line and segment sections contain **Overall** and six four-hour columns.

Line rankings use the nearest-rank 30th percentile of usable segment percentages, favoring routes with broadly high occupancy instead of one isolated maximum. Segment Overall rankings use each directed segment's highest usable period.

The overview shows up to three lines and ten segments per column. Select a heading for a detailed ranking of up to 200 entries, then choose most-used-first or least-used-first sorting.

Selecting a line shield opens that route. Selecting a segment shield focuses its departure stop. Percentages are capped at 100%, and entries without recorded capacity are omitted.
