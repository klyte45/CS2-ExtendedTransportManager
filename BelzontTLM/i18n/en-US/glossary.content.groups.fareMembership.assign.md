For an unmanaged passenger line, open **Ticket Price** in the Selected Info Panel and use its gear menu to assign a fare group. A managed line's menu can edit its group, remove the line, or move it to another group.

The group editor separates **Lines in this group** from **Available lines to link to this group**. Add or remove one line, remove all members, or add every line remaining after passenger transport-type, service-state, and assigned-lines filters.

A line can belong to only one fare group. A warning marks lines already assigned elsewhere; adding one here immediately replaces its previous membership. Inactive passenger lines remain assignable. Cargo routes are not listed.

Removing membership leaves the last group-applied ticket price on the line. Adjust it manually if needed.
