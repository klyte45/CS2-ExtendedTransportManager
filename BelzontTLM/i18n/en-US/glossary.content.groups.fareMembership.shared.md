For a managed line, **Ticket Price** shows the fare-group name, current effective fare, and number of lines affected by shared changes. Its tooltip lists member identifiers and names, shortening large groups to five names plus an overflow count.

The normal per-line slider is replaced by this summary. Use the gear button to edit the group, remove this line, or move it elsewhere.

Changing a group's default fare or exceptions affects every member. Membership and fare edits save automatically; there is no confirmation dialog or separate Save button. Review the affected count, members, and schedule before editing.

Invalid or overlapping exception ranges block the whole group update until corrected.
