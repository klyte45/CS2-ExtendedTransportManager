**District boundaries** starts enabled and can be toggled from the district button on the XTM linear-map toolbar.

The overlay is schematic: horizontal dashed separators mark places where consecutive displayed stops resolve to different districts. The labels above and below a separator name the district being left and the district being entered. They are not geographic district outlines.

XTM normally takes a station or building's current district. For parentless roadside stops, it uses a district bordering the attached road edge. Stops that cannot be assigned to a district are labeled **<Districtless>** in gray.
