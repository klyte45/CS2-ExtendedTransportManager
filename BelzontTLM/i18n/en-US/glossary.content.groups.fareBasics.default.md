The **Default fare** is used by every member line whenever no hour exception covers the current game-clock hour.

A new group starts with the game's normal default ticket fare. Positive values below the game's permitted minimum may be raised to that minimum when saved.

Set the fare to **0** for free travel. XTM displays zero as **Free** and disables the ticket-price policy on member lines while zero is effective.

An exception can still charge during part of a free-default day, or create a free period within a paid schedule. Valid changes save automatically and propagate to all current members.
