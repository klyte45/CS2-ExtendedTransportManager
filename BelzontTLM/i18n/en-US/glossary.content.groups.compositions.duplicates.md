Two compositions cannot contain the same exact primary-and-secondary pair. Choices that would reproduce another pair are disabled, and repeated pairs are also removed during backend validation.

The same primary can appear with different carriages, and one carriage can be paired with different primary vehicles.

Without a carriage picker, the limit equals the number of primary vehicles. With one, it equals primary vehicles multiplied by every carriage plus the no-carriage choice. The heading shows the current count and limit; **Add composition** disables at the limit.

Empty rows count while editing but are omitted when saved. Sort model cards by name, capacity, or total length; selecting the active sort reverses it.
