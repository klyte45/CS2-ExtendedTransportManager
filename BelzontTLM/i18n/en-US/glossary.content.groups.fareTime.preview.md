Expand **Fare schedule** below the default-fare field. The first row shows the default; remaining rows show each inclusive exception range and fare, sorted by starting hour. Zero appears as **Free**.

During an exception range, that fare replaces the default. During every unlisted hour, the default applies.

The foldout summarizes configured exceptions; it is not a complete 24-hour timetable. Remember that an end hour includes the whole hour through minute 59.

XTM reevaluates the effective fare when the game enters a new hour and applies a changed value to every member line.
