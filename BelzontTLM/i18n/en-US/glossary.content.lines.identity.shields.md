XTM shields combine a route's transport type, current color, display identifier, cargo mode, and service state. Most XTM views place the display identifier inside the shield, falling back to the internal route number. Listing cards instead show the identifier beside a shield containing the transport icon.

## Shapes

Passenger buses use a hexagon, trams a trapezoid, ferries a diamond, and subway routes a square. Train routes use a circle. Ships and airplanes use their own pointed shapes. Cargo train, ship, and airplane routes retain the corresponding transport shape.

The shield fill uses the route's current color. Identifier text and the optional listing border use a contrasting light or dark color, and long text is reduced to fit on one line.

## Badges

Day-and-night service has no schedule badge. Day-only routes receive a yellow circle, night-only routes a purple circle, and disabled routes a red circle at the lower-left. Cargo routes also receive a beige circle at the lower-right. These badges are color markers without icons.
