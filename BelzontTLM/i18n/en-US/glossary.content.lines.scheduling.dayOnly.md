## Daytime service

**Day only** keeps the route enabled and assigns the game's daytime schedule. The simulation's day/night cycle controls when service runs; XTM does not define or display custom opening hours for an individual route.

Select the second button in a card's schedule strip to enable the route and apply Day-only service. This also replaces any previously stored Night or Day & night schedule.

## How it appears

Day-only cards use pale-yellow information text. Their selected schedule button is yellow, and XTM shields receive a yellow circular badge at the upper-left.

The **Day only** toolbar button includes or excludes these routes from the listing. Occupancy reports also allow active Day-only routes to be isolated.
