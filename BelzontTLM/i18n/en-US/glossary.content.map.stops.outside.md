XTM recognizes an outside connection when the route's connected stop entity has the game's outside-connection component.

With **District boundaries** enabled, XTM draws district separators around an outside-connection stop. If that stop has no resolved district, its boundary label is shown in gold as **Colossal Nation: {stop name}**. The stop marker itself is otherwise drawn like an ordinary stop.

Outside-connection status does not change route order or automatically make the stop a terminus. In symmetric half-trip mode, an outside connection is visible only when it belongs to the displayed half of the route.
