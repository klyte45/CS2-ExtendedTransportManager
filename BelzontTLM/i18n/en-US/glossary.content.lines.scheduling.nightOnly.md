## Nighttime service

**Night only** keeps the route enabled and assigns the game's nighttime schedule. The simulation's night period controls when service runs; XTM does not add a separate per-route timetable.

Select the third button in a card's schedule strip to enable the route and apply Night-only service. This replaces any previously stored Day or Day & night schedule.

## How it appears

Night-only cards use pale-purple information text. Their selected schedule button is purple, and XTM shields receive a purple circular badge at the upper-left.

The **Night only** toolbar button can isolate these routes in the listing. Active Night-only routes can also be isolated in passenger or cargo occupancy reports.
