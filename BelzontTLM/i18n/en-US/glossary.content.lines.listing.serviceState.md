## Four service states

The listing recognizes **Day & night**, **Day only**, **Night only**, and **Disabled**.

A line marked inactive is always classified as Disabled, even if it still stores a previous day or night schedule. Active lines use their schedule to determine one of the other three states.

## Filtering by service state

Each state button works as an inclusion toggle. Selected states remain visible; deselected states are hidden. Service-state filters combine with transport-mode filters, so a card must pass both sets of choices.

The **Passenger lines** and **Cargo routes** presets preserve the current service-state selection. **Show all** and **Hide all** reset both filter families.

## Changing service from a card

Every card has four schedule buttons in this order: Day & night, Day only, Night only, and Disabled. Selecting a schedule enables the line and applies that schedule. Selecting Disabled stops service without deleting the route or changing its path.

Disabled cards are gray, show their length, and replace the normal vehicle and occupancy information with **Line disabled**. Day-only cards use a pale-yellow tint, night-only cards use purple, and day-and-night cards use the normal light text.
