**Half trip** starts enabled, but applies only when XTM detects a symmetric out-and-back route and vehicles are hidden. Detection compares stops at mirrored route positions by their parent station or building.

The shortened map runs from the route's first stop to its midpoint terminus. Intermediate outbound and return platforms sharing a parent appear as split stop markers. Hover over a split marker to compare waiting passengers or cargo and platform crowding for both directions.

Distances describe the displayed direction. Segment occupancy can show both directions with down and up arrows. Connecting-line shields remain available, although they are taken from the displayed platform rather than merged from its paired return platform.

Enable vehicles or turn off **Half trip** to display the complete loop. Enabling vehicles does not clear the Half-trip selection, so the shortened view returns automatically when vehicles are hidden again. Changing the route's first stop can change whether its mirrored stop order is detected.
