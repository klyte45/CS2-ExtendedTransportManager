Enable vehicle markers and select one to open **Vehicle Data**. **Odometer** is the distance recorded for that vehicle. **Maintenance range** is the maintenance-distance interval defined by its vehicle model. Both values use your selected measurement units.

When the route itself is selected, expand **Line Data** and then **Advanced data** to see the vehicle with the least calculated distance remaining before its maintenance range. Select its name to focus that vehicle.

XTM reports this information but does not send vehicles to a depot or perform maintenance. A negative remaining distance means the vehicle has passed its reported maintenance range.
