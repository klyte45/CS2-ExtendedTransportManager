## Passenger and cargo are separate filters

Passenger **lines** and cargo **routes** have separate mode buttons. A selected button is included in the results; a deselected button is excluded.

Use individual mode buttons when you want a precise combination, such as passenger buses together with cargo trains.

## Quick filters

**Passenger lines** hides every cargo mode currently present and re-enables all passenger modes. **Cargo routes** does the opposite. These presets keep your day, night, and disabled service-state choices unchanged.

Because a quick filter rebuilds the mode selection for its side, it also clears any individual mode exclusions previously made on that side.

**Show all** clears every transport-mode and service-state exclusion. **Hide all** excludes all represented modes and all service states.

The count at the right reports the cards that remain after both kinds of filter are applied. If the city has routes but the current combination excludes all of them, the listing shows **No matching lines**.
