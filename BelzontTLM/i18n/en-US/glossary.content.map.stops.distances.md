**Distances** starts enabled and can be toggled from the ruler button on the XTM linear-map toolbar.

A label shows the route-path length from one displayed stop to the next. XTM adds the path lengths of the route segments between those stops and formats the result in the current measurement system. When the interval crosses intermediate route waypoints, the label also shows their count as `wp`.

In symmetric half-trip mode, distances describe only the displayed direction from the first stop to the midpoint terminus. Hide vehicles and keep **Half trip** enabled to use that view; turn off **Half trip** to inspect distances around the complete loop.
