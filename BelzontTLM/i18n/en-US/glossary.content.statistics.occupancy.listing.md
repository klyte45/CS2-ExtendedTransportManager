Each enabled route card shows a historical occupancy range beside its vehicle count.

The first percentage is the lowest usable occupancy across every directed segment and four-hour period on the route; the second is the highest. Periods older than yesterday are ignored. The background color follows the maximum value.

A new route or route without usable history displays **0.0%~0.0%**, which looks the same as genuinely empty usable history. Disabled cards show **Line disabled** instead of vehicle and occupancy information.

![Enabled passenger and cargo cards showing their historical occupancy ranges, plus a disabled card](coui://xtm.k45/UI/images/xtm-line-card-anatomy.jpg)

Choose **Line usage (%)** under **Sort lines** to order each transport-type group by maximum non-stale occupancy. The primary direction places the highest maximum first. If refreshed values make the order appear stale, select the sort again.

![Sort lines menu with Line usage selected](coui://xtm.k45/UI/images/xtm-listing-sort-menu.jpg)
