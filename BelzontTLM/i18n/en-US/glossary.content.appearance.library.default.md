XTM includes a read-only preset library organized into folders by region and collection. Browse it through **Default library** or the **XTM: Library** bookmark in the palette picker.

Importing a preset creates a separate city palette that can be renamed and edited. Its city name uses the preset's final filename without the library path. You can also append a preset's colors to an existing city palette.

Editing the city copy never changes the bundled preset.
