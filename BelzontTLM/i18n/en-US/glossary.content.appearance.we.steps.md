A destination blind is a sequence of steps along the selected route. Each step is used until a chosen stop, or through the **end of the line**.

XTM selects the first step whose destination still lies ahead. Boarding advances the choice one stop early. A final step with no destination covers the rest of the trip.

New lines normally receive a mid-route step and an end-of-line step. Both contain destination-or-district text followed by the route number. **Reset to defaults** rebuilds this pattern.

Add, edit, reorder, or remove steps; at least one must remain. Saving sorts destination steps along the route and places end-of-line last.

Changing the route's **first stop** renumbers boundaries. End-of-line destination names resolve from the route's current first stop.
