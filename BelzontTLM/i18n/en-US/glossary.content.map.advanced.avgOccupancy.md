Under **Advanced stats**, XTM shows two live percentages for the selected route.

**Average vehicle occupancy** is the mean of each positioned vehicle's current load divided by its capacity. **Average stops occupancy** is the mean of each stop's waiting load divided by the largest vehicle capacity on the route, which is also the capacity proxy used by the linear map's platform-crowding display.

Below those rows, **Historical occupancy** charts the selected route's recorded segment occupancy by four-hour block. Its daily average ignores stale blocks. This history belongs only to the selected route and is separate from the city Occupancy Report.

A route with no positioned vehicles can leave the live averages empty or meaningless until vehicles appear.
