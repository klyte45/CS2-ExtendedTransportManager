Generated keyframes can add optional **Prefix** and **Suffix** around their text. Static-text keyframes use one fixed-text field instead.

**Copy to clipboard** stores the route's complete blind configuration inside XTM for the current UI session. This is not the operating-system clipboard and does not persist between sessions.

**Erase all and paste from clipboard** replaces every step on the open route. Destination stops remap by relative progress along the new route, while end-of-line remains end-of-line. Paste is disabled until something has been copied.

**Reset to defaults** discards the current configuration and restores the generated mid-route and end-of-line steps for this route.
