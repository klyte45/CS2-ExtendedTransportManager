Each stop is labeled with the name resolved by the game for that stop entity. Hover over its marker to see the name of its main building or station when XTM finds a parent.

Select a stop on the linear map to focus the stop itself and open **Stop Data**. The building button there focuses the top-level building or station that owns the platform; it is separate from selecting the stop.
