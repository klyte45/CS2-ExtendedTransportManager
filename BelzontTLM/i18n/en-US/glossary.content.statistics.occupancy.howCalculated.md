XTM records occupancy after boarding events and stores peaks into six four-hour buckets plus current-hour and daily-average views. Listing “usage” sorting leans on historical peak behavior, while city rankings may use percentile summaries.

Missing buckets are skipped internally; empty charts usually mean the line has not run long enough in that window.
